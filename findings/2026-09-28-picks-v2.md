## How v2 was built

1. **Search.** Four research helpers, one per category, each asked for 5 candidates that could
   pass a VALUATION bar, not just a story screen, plus a dated break condition for each.
2. **Data.** Prices from a live quote; share counts from SEC filings (cover page, or diluted count
   for multi-class companies). Bel Fuse is the only estimate. `shares.py` now REFUSES stale
   counts after it silently returned 2009-2011 figures for Comcast, Accenture and Bel Fuse.
3. **Gate 1: valuation.** `scripts/screen.py` on `data/screen-inputs-2026-09-28.csv`. One exit
   multiple for every stock (16x, your market-average rule), also run at 12x and 20x.
   **PASS** = base case above 12%/yr at 12x, 16x AND 20x. **CLEAR FAIL** = below 12% even at 20x.
   Anything between is **UNRESOLVED**: the model can't tell.
4. **Gate 2: thesis.** A cheap price is often the market pricing a real risk. A name that passes
   Gate 1 still fails if that risk is visibly happening now, or if the thesis has no measured
   evidence.
5. **Crash record.** Every final pick is checked against the Nasdaq's big falls from real price
   history, not memory.

## FINAL PICKS (passed both gates)

### 1. BKNG — Booking Holdings · kept from v1
**Data:** $164.55 [S]; 751.38M shares [S] → ~$124B; revenue $26.9B FY25, ~$28.5B trailing [V];
FCF $9.1B [V]; operating margin ~21% (FY21) → 34.5% (FY25) [G/V].
**Measured AI evidence:** customer-service cost down while volume +10%; voice AI takes most calls;
savings target $650M [V].
**What the low multiple prices [O]:** AI assistants taking over trip search. Instrument: AI
referrals as a share of room nights, under 1% today [V].
**Thesis:** the clearest cost line bent by internal AI, kept as margin, with distribution AI
can't copy.
**Cases [C]:** bear 7.8% · base 16.5% (14.3-18.4) · bull 21.2%.
**Crash record [S]:** 2007-09 -16% (Nasdaq -56%) · 2020 -41% (-30%) · 2021-22 -16% (-36%) ·
2024-25 -19% (-24%). In the dot-com bust (as Priceline) it fell 99%.
**Break:** take rate falls two years running, or AI referrals pass 10% of room nights. Next
check: Q3 report, late Oct 2026.

### 2. EXPE — Expedia Group · NEW
**Data:** $265.00 [S]; 123.76M diluted shares [S] → ~$32.8B; net debt ~$1B excluding customer
float [G]; revenue ~$15.5B trailing [G], 2026 guide up to $16.22B [V]; trailing FCF $4.5B [V],
inflated by prepaid-booking float, which the base case removes (22% margin vs ~29% trailing) [C].
**Measured evidence:** Q2 revenue +14% with overhead flat year on year [V]; EBITDA margin 25.9%,
150-175bp expansion guided for 2026 [V]. Only partly attributable to AI; AI token costs rise in
the second half [V].
**What the low multiple prices [O]:** the same risk as Booking (Google and AI agents taking the
search step) plus float-inflated cash flow. Expedia is the cheaper, lower-quality version of the
Booking thesis.
**Thesis:** it owns loyalty, supply contracts and B2B travel plumbing; revenue grows while overhead
doesn't, so savings stay as margin; about 7x trailing FCF.
**Cases [C]:** bear 13.6% · base 21.3% (19.1-23.1) · bull 27.0%. Even the bear case clears 12%.
**Crash record [S]:** 2007-09 -80% · 2020 -58% · 2021-22 -52% · 2024-25 -25%. It falls harder than
the Nasdaq: this is a cyclical travel stock, not a hedge.
**Break:** Q3 (~Nov 6, 2026) shows overhead growing faster than revenue, or the margin guide cut.
**Sizing note [O]:** Booking and Expedia are one bet (online travel vs AI agents). Size them
together, not as two picks.

### 3. CI — Cigna Group · NEW (found independently by two helpers)
**Data:** $272.40 [S]; 264.24M shares [S] → ~$72B; net debt ~$25B [G]; revenue ~$275-285B [V/G];
FCF $8.4B FY25 [V]; 2026 EPS guide raised to at least $30.45 [V].
**Measured AI evidence:** inbound calls -20% at Cigna Healthcare and -25% at Evernorth pharmacy vs.
two years ago [V]; adjusted SG&A ratio 5.8% → 4.8% [V] (partly revenue mix).
**Forced vs. fundamental:** estimates went UP (guide raised, Q2 beat) while the price fell to ~9x
earnings [V/G]. That's the asymmetry lens: fundamentals intact, price down on policy fear.
**What the low multiple prices [O]:** pharmacy-benefit (PBM) reform squeezing Evernorth's margin.
That risk is real and legislative, so it's the thing to watch, not dismiss.
**Thesis:** a very large revenue base where small AI-driven cost ratios become billions, at ~9-10x
FCF, with estimates rising while the price falls.
**Cases [C]:** bear 8.2% · base 14.3% (12.2-16.1) · bull 18.7%. Passes, but with the least margin
of the final picks.
**Crash record [S]:** 2000-02 -7% (Nasdaq -78%) · 2007-09 -74% · 2020 -41% · 2021-22 +61% ·
2024-25 +15%. Same shape as Berkshire: holds in a tech bust and rate shocks, not in panics.
**Break:** Q3 (~Oct 30, 2026) cuts the $30.45 floor, SG&A returns above 5.5%, or Evernorth misses
its guide of at least $6.9B adjusted operating income; the Feb 2027 outlook below ~$33.

### 4. LMB — Limbach Holdings · NEW (a picks-and-shovels name, NOT a titan)
**Data:** $51.48 [S]; 11.92M shares [S] → ~$614M; net debt $24M [V]; 2026 revenue guide
$760-790M [V]; Q2 revenue +21.9% [V]; Q2 FCF $13.7M [V], ~$55M trailing [G].
**Why it isn't a titan:** gross margin FALLING 28% → 21.5% [V]; insiders only ~2-3% [G]. Two of
the nine traits fail.
**Why it's here:** skilled mechanical and electrical labor is a real bottleneck; the CYMCOR purchase
moves it earlier into data-center projects; management says it competes for workers on data-center
jobs [V]. About 10x FCF after a guidance reset.
**Ground truth you can check:** mechanical and electrical subcontractors on data-center and
industrial sites near you, and whether crews are short-handed.
**What the low multiple prices [O]:** margin pressure. Break condition is exactly that.
**Cases [C]:** bear 10.1% · base 20.7% (18.4-22.5) · bull 29.7%.
**Crash record [S]:** 2020 -42% · 2021-22 +48% · 2024-25 -24% (listed ~2014). Small and volatile.
**Break:** Q3 (early Nov 2026) gross margin below 21%, or adjusted EBITDA guide below $78M.
**Size [O]:** small. It's a ~$600M construction contractor.
