## 3. Four-step valuation method (user's)
1. Market cap -> enterprise value; net debt in years of FCF (~2.5 reasonable).
2. Eight pillars over 5-10 yrs: 5yr P/E, 5yr P/FCF, ROIC, revenue growth, profit margin, FCF growth, share count, debt.
3. 10-yr low/mid/high: revenue growth, FCF margin, exit multiple (market avg 15-16).
4. 9% = fair value; 12% = margin of safety via higher required return.
