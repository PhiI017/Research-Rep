# My companies and my thesis for each

From my notes of 2026-09-27. Test status and evidence live in `REGISTER.md`.

## My own theses

**Amazon** — AI automation and robotics winner.
Amazon's consumer side runs on thin margins because it takes hundreds of thousands of
expensive workers. It is already the industry leader, has spent 20+ years building global
logistics and infrastructure, and is a household name. As robotics and automation roll out at
scale, labor costs fall and margins expand sharply, which lifts the stock. Distribution is the
moat that AI can't copy: once intelligence is cheap for everyone, whoever owns logistics wins.

**Robinhood** — wins the next generation of investors.
More people are investing than ever, especially young people, and Robinhood is how my
generation sees investing: the app, the brand, prediction markets. As those customers age and
the huge inheritances arrive, Robinhood is positioned to capture a big share of that money.
Separate from AI.
